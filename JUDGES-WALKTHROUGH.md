# Seer / Signal — review walkthrough

Created by [@0xterricola](https://x.com/0xterricola). All examples and transactions are simulated.

1. **[Seer homepage](https://0xterricola.github.io/seer-signal/):** switch the application lenses in the hero. Each previews the kind of question its application answers.
2. **[Seer app / Explorer](https://0xterricola.github.io/seer-signal/explorer/):** inspect a market and expand its resolution context. Filter by market type to see how units and simulations adapt.
3. **[Deep](https://0xterricola.github.io/seer-signal/deep/):** load sample agent forecasts. The disagreement map reveals differences; filter gaps of at least five points and review the batch, ordered by disagreement with liquidity alongside. Edit a score and navigate away and back to see draft persistence.
4. **[Futarchy](https://0xterricola.github.io/seer-signal/futarchy/):** switch proposals and compare pass/fail forecasts on the same success measure. The DAO retains the decision.
5. **[Opportunity Markets](https://0xterricola.github.io/seer-signal/opportunity/):** inspect the distinct submission briefs and shortlist candidates. Review the rubric before simulating a selection forecast.
6. **[Risk Pricing](https://0xterricola.github.io/seer-signal/risk/):** select two or three protocols and compare their annualized estimates, definitions, liquidity, and activity.

Try the light theme and a narrow window. [Design notes](https://0xterricola.github.io/seer-signal/design/) explain the Electric Foundation adaptations.

## Repository and deployment

The source is available in [0xterricola/seer-signal](https://github.com/0xterricola/seer-signal). The demos are deployed on GitHub Pages: source is maintained on `main`, and the contents of `site/` are published from `gh-pages`. No build step is required. See the [README](README.md#publish-updates) for update commands.

The owner confirmed that the deployed site loads and looks good. This does not establish a complete production-origin or real-device test pass; the [final audit](FINAL-AUDIT.md) records the limits of verification.
