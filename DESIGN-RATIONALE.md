# Seer / Signal

X: @0xterricola

## Short rationale

Seer / Signal turns Electric Foundation into a connected product system. Violet, dark neutral surfaces, and compact controls create continuity; each interface changes its layout to match the question being asked. The homepage explains the ecosystem. Explorer makes every market discoverable. Deep supports forecasts across many candidates. Futarchy compares conditional futures on one scale. Opportunity helps reviewers inspect and shortlist work. Risk Pricing keeps estimates comparable without hiding liquidity or activity.

The common principle is that market predictions inform human judgment. Units, resolution context, and interpretation sit beside the forecast rather than being buried in a disclaimer.

## Deliberate departures from EF

The supplied EF board guided the electric violet, near-black background, neutral surface layers, and dark/light treatment. This prototype deliberately adds:

- Space Grotesk headings and DM Sans interface text for hierarchy and readability. These are selected implementation fonts, not verified EF font assets. Both are bundled locally as WOFF2 files with their open-source licenses.
- A persistent ecosystem navigation rail, so moving between products remains predictable.
- More whitespace and larger section headings, balanced with compact data rows.
- Tables for comparative tasks, independent cards for reviewing submissions, and shared-scale charts for conditional forecasts.
- Restrained green activity and amber liquidity indicators, always accompanied by text.
- A simplified text wordmark and an eye favicon. These are prototype adaptations rather than official exported EF logo files. The homepage uses an interactive product preview to explain the ecosystem.

The design stays within EF’s visual character while adapting its components to six different jobs.

## Six useful walkthroughs

1. Homepage: switch between five product previews, then follow the corresponding link into an application.
2. Explorer: search for “science”, filter market types, star a market, inspect a binary market, and simulate binary, categorical, scalar, conditional, or independent-selection positions. Scalar and conditional simulations include an adjustable settlement example. Invalid amounts are rejected.
3. Deep: import a CSV with candidate_id and score, edit forecasts, compare them on the disagreement map, filter gaps of five points or more, and review a batch ordered by largest disagreement. Sample forecasts fill empty slots without replacing existing valid scores; drafts can be exported or cleared. Valid drafts persist across navigation; invalid scores block batch review. Scores are independent 0–100 evaluator estimates, not a distribution that must sum to 100%.
4. Futarchy: switch between three proposals. Read both branches on the same contributor scale, inspect liquidity and interpretation, and save a branch for review. The forecast gap is not presented as a guaranteed causal effect or a governance vote.
5. Opportunity Markets: shortlist submissions, inspect the panel’s rubric, and simulate a Yes/No selection position. Up to three entries can be selected; market rank does not determine selection.
6. Risk Pricing: filter protocols, compare two or three estimates, inspect common definitions and thin liquidity, and export the filtered table. The annualized risk estimate is never relabelled as quarterly event probability.
